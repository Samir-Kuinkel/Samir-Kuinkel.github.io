STL Wasp Watch is a community-science project I founded to engage local residents in documenting paper wasp populations across the St. Louis region. Participants record sightings of native and invasive wasps, contributing observations that help map distributions and track the spread of the invasive European paper wasp (*Polistes dominula*).

The project turns everyday observations into usable ecological data while making science accessible and hands-on for the public — connecting my research on invasive species and climate change to the community it affects.
