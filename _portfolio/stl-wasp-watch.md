STL Wasp Watch is a community-science project I created where local residents can document paper wasp  across the St. Louis region. Participants can record sightings of native and invasive wasps, around their local ares which would be helpful to map distributions and track the spread of the invasive European paper wasp (*Polistes dominula*).

The project connects local community to the scientific reserach , making community people at the front of scienetifc reserach. This would turn  the simple observtaion into usable ecological data while making science accessible and hands-on for the public  connecting my research on invasive species and climate change to the community it affects.
