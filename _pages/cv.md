Research Interests
======
Invasion biology, biogeography, and ecological niche modeling, with a secondary focus on urban thermal ecology and the macroecological morphology of social insects. My research integrates large-scale occurrence data, species distribution modeling, computer vision, and physiological experiments to understand how species respond to environmental change.

Research Experience
======
* **Graduate Research Assistant**, Social Insect Diversity Lab, UMSL — Aug 2023 – Present (Advisor: Dr. Sara E. Miller)
  * *Niche dynamics & invasion biology:* Compiled and cleaned 82,058 occurrence records for 16 Polistes species (GBIF, iNaturalist); ran comparative niche-overlap and native–invasive niche dynamics analyses for P. dominula using the COUE framework; quantified climatic novelty with MESS analyses and identified climatic and anthropogenic drivers of expansion.
  * *Urban thermal biology:* Deployed temperature loggers at 60 nest sites across the St. Louis urban heat gradient; ran CTmax/CTmin thermal-tolerance experiments on 385 individuals; performed landscape analyses in Google Earth Engine; optimized RNA extraction for RNA-seq.
  * *Macroecological morphology:* Built a four-stage computer-vision pipeline (color calibration, object detection, instance segmentation, color extraction) to quantify body coloration from museum specimens.
* **Master's Researcher**, Christian-Albrechts-Universität, Kiel — Jul 2021 – May 2022
  * Designed CRISPR-Cas9 guide RNAs targeting SOC1 and SPL5 homeologs in *Brassica napus*; performed Agrobacterium-mediated transformation and molecular validation of edits.
